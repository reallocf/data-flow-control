# Assessment scope and statutory review

## Safety achievement awards

Section 274(j)(4)(C) distinguishes two conditions:

1. Safety achievement awards previously awarded by the
   employer during the taxable year to more than ten
   percent of eligible employees, excluding awards
   described in section 132(e)(1).

2. Awards to managers, administrators, clerical
   employees or other professional employees.

A recipient's prior safety award is not itself a
distinct automatic statutory disqualification.

The employee-level award history and employer-level
percentage restriction must not be conflated.

The existing A07 assessment criterion requires
clarification during independent rubric review.
Historical scores remain unchanged.

## Gift amount conditions

Actual prior qualifying gift expenses are distinct
from independently committed deduction decisions.

An annual sum of proposed deductions alone does not
establish every statutory amount condition.
