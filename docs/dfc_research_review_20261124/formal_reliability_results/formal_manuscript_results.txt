FORMAL POLICY RELIABILITY EVALUATION

Methods

The experiment comprised thirty new model responses
across six conditions, with five repetitions per
condition. Section 274 concerned ordinary gifts,
cruise convention expenses and employee achievement
awards. Section 195 provided a distinct statutory
semantic task.

The assessment retained seventeen criteria for
Section 274 and ten criteria for Section 195.
Each criterion received a score of zero, one or two
for absent or incorrect, partial, or complete
semantic content respectively.

The response was the experimental repetition unit.
Complete criterion coverage and pairwise Jaccard
similarity provided distinct measures of completeness
and consistency.

Results

All thirty planned responses were obtained.
The assessment covered 440 criterion instances:
108 complete, 220 partial and 112 zero.

For Section 274, Qwen scored 73/170 under condition A
and 64/170 under condition B. GPT-OSS scored 69/170
and 74/170 respectively.

The explicit annual preparation environment did not
produce a consistent improvement across both models.

For Section 195, Qwen scored 73/100 and GPT-OSS
scored 83/100.

Mean pairwise Jaccard similarity ranged from
approximately 0.183 to 0.670 across the six conditions.

Limitations

The criteria address a restricted statutory scope
rather than complete tax policy recall.

The semantic assessment was model-assisted.
Independent statutory assessment remains pending.

Five repetitions per condition support descriptive
comparisons but do not establish a general model
ranking or a population-level causal effect.

The assessment retained visible content from one
truncated response and included sensitivity analysis.

The format diagnostics do not establish complete
schema validity or DFC implementation correctness.
